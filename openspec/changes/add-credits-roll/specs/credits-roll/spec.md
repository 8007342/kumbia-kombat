## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Credits come from the real dependency data

The credits SHALL be generated by a committed script from the project's own dependency records:
the Dart lock file and package graph, the mixer's Cargo lock, the Python tools' imports and
environments, the node scripts and `package.json`, and the committed model and engine provenance,
plus a hand-kept list for the base and the tools no lock file names. Every credited item SHALL carry
its degree: 0 for the base (Tillandsias, Podman, Fedora), 1 for what the project uses directly, 2 for
what those use, and so on. The generated file SHALL be committed, and SHALL be regenerated when
dependencies change; a test SHALL fail when a lock file names a package the credits don't.

#### Scenario: a new Dart package

- **WHEN** a pull request adds a package to `pubspec.lock` without regenerating the credits
- **THEN** the credits test fails and names the package

#### Scenario: a source that can't be read

- **WHEN** the script runs where the forge's venvs don't exist
- **THEN** it still writes the file, and lists the venvs under its sources as not read

### Requirement: The roll scrolls the credits up, nearest first and largest

The credits page SHALL play music while the text scrolls up, headed "Built using" and
"Tillandsias.org", then the base, then direct dependencies in one column in large text, then
second-degree dependencies in more columns in smaller text, then third-degree and deeper in more
columns still and smaller again. A drag SHALL hold the roll. A close control SHALL leave it.

#### Scenario: sizes by degree

- **WHEN** the roll shows a direct dependency, a second-degree one and a fifth-degree one
- **THEN** each is smaller than the one before, and the deeper ones sit in narrower columns

#### Scenario: a phone

- **WHEN** the shipping credits roll on a 360 by 740 screen
- **THEN** every item lays out without overflow

### Requirement: Where the credits play

The credits SHALL roll after the campaign's last end screen, after a pause long enough to read it,
and return to that screen by themselves when they end. A level played alone SHALL NOT roll them. When
the VIP finale exists, they SHALL roll after it instead. They SHALL also open at `/credits/`,
`/?page=credits` and `/#/credits`, and the console SHALL link to them.

#### Scenario: the campaign ends

- **WHEN** the last room's success music finishes and the end screen shows
- **THEN** about 8 seconds later the credits roll, and when they have run out they return to the end screen

#### Scenario: from the console

- **WHEN** the owner taps CREDITS in the console's campaign card
- **THEN** the browser opens `/credits/`

### Requirement: Credits music

The roll SHALL play credits music, looping until it ends. Until credits music is made, it SHALL play
a rendered take the game already ships, and the credits file SHALL mark it as a stand-in. When the
browser holds sound back, the page SHALL offer a control to start it.

#### Scenario: stand-in music

- **WHEN** the credits roll before credits music exists
- **THEN** they play the stand-in take on a loop, and the data says it is a stand-in
