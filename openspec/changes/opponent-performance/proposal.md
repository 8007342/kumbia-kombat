# Proposal

## Why

Mācron now has authored movement phases and music-linked turns, while opponents still read as disconnected cue poses. Their floor handoffs lack visible, grounded walk cycles, and most of the audience cannot lose interest after poor play. Durable behavior and art contracts will guide both runtime changes and generated animation assets.

## What Changes

- Define a shared opponent performance vocabulary for cue-in, anticipation, support, accent, follow-through, settle, transitions, and character-specific secondary action.
- Require chart-faithful calls and progressively coordinated authored motifs, rather than arbitrary pose changes or decorative jumping.
- Require complete, beat-sampled approach, dance-area handoff, exit, facing, and depth-scaled locomotion.
- Make audience interest respond in both directions to phrase results, with slow room-wide loss and stronger local setbacks, preserving caps and animated stand/sit transitions.
- Keep the animation research and its project-specific applications in a cited register for future image-generation work.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `art-direction`: add opponent motion, locomotion, grounding, and opponent-art acceptance requirements.
- `rhythm-combat`: define chart-matched opponent choreography and reversible crowd response to play quality.

## Impact

Affected behavior includes opponent pose selection and scene staging, crowd interest and transitions, art metadata, cloud/ComfyUI asset-generation prompts, and phone-size animation review. Existing judgment, input windows, chart timing, crowd caps, and room progression remain authoritative.
