# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Opponent choreography teaches authored chart complexity

An opponent's call SHALL demonstrate the exact active chart limbs and event beats. Across round tiers, the authored chart SHALL introduce more coordinated or rhythmically varied motifs only after simpler patterns have been taught. Visual busyness, random pose changes, or animation speed alone SHALL not represent increased gameplay complexity. Character-specific style SHALL shape the movement while preserving the chart's required limb identity, beat, one-thumb reach, and input spacing.

#### Scenario: higher-tier motif

- **WHEN** a player reaches a higher authored tier
- **THEN** the opponent demonstrates the new coordination or rhythm before the response is judged
- **AND** the cue sequence and opponent accent markers agree on limb identity and event timing.

#### Scenario: call and response remain musically identical

- **WHEN** the opponent demonstrates a chart phrase and the player receives its response
- **THEN** every required limb and beat remains the same between call and response
- **AND** decorative follow-through does not add, move, or conceal a required input.

### Requirement: Phrase results change crowd interest in both directions

Phrase success SHALL raise guest interest gradually within room ceilings. A failed phrase SHALL lower interest for nearby guests; repeated consecutive failures SHALL also lower interest across the wider audience. Interest thresholds SHALL use hysteresis.

#### Scenario: setbacks reduce broad enthusiasm

- **WHEN** the player fails successive response phrases
- **THEN** guest interest progressively falls across the room after repeated setbacks, with nearby guests reacting more strongly
- **AND** affected dancers finish any active stand-up motion before playing a sit-down transition.

#### Scenario: recovered performance rebuilds interest

- **WHEN** the player resumes passing phrases after a setback
- **THEN** crowd interest rises gradually again under the same individual thresholds and room ceilings
- **AND** guests transition through authored attention and standing motions rather than snapping into dance.

### Requirement: Crowd state transitions preserve earned response rules

Standing guests losing interest SHALL complete an authored sit-down transition. Temporary flourishes MAY cause local reactions but SHALL not create lasting interest or bypass room ceilings. Crowd presentation SHALL not change scoring, progression, or clearance requirements.

#### Scenario: flourish is temporary

- **WHEN** a player performs a flourish
- **THEN** nearby guests may briefly react
- **AND** their persistent interest and room participation ceilings remain unchanged.
