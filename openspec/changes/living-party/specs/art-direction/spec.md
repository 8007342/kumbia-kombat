## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Asset acceptance
Art SHALL be inspected at native resolution, on a physical portrait phone,
against all five palettes. Sheets require frame metadata, loop tags, anchor,
facing, license/provenance and palette. A concept sheet is not a playable sprite
atlas until frame cleanup and identity checks are complete. Animation markers
SHALL be driven by musical beat or gameplay state; cosmetic animation cannot
change judgment time.

Runtime art SHALL use a coherent native pixel grid, grounded pivots and authored
multi-frame movement. Sprite metadata SHALL include frame rectangles, facing,
nonuniform holds, contact/accent markers and transition poses as applicable.
Concept sheets and flattened background paintings SHALL remain starters until
cleaned and reviewed. Symphony of the Night is motion inspiration; the original
party theme, character canon and warm material language SHALL remain.

#### Scenario: runtime atlas acceptance

- **WHEN** an atlas is registered for a level
- **THEN** its frame metadata, provenance and native-resolution character review SHALL pass before runtime use.

#### Scenario: dance proof
Given an authored required gesture and turn, when reviewed in motion at native
size, weight transfer, supporting foot contact, facing changes and secondary
settling SHALL be readable without continuous distortion of a static body.

#### Scenario: reactive accepted input
Given an accepted early or late tap, the dancer SHALL respond immediately using
the accepted event timestamp. Early preparation MAY reach an accent at the
scheduled note; late input SHALL NOT require an accent in the past. Animation
SHALL NOT change judgment timestamps, windows or immutable cue destinations.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: gradually earned individual crowd response
Guests SHALL begin occupied and gain lasting interest primarily through completed
required responses. Individual hits and optional flourishes MAY trigger brief
local reactions but SHALL NOT shortcut sustained room participation. Neighbor
influence SHALL be bounded and SHALL NOT earn interest merely through elapsed
time. Good and Perfect required hits SHALL support the same complete social arc.
Crowd states SHALL NOT become an additional progression gate.

#### Scenario: first phrase
Given six successful taps in the first response, scattered guests MAY glance
toward Mācron, but the whole crowd SHALL NOT immediately cheer. A clean taps-only
run SHALL gradually earn the complete welcoming payoff at actual clearance.

#### Scenario: miss and replay
Given earned interest, one miss SHALL NOT synchronously erase it. Pausing and
replaying a response SHALL restore a defined snapshot and SHALL NOT duplicate
committed gains. Completed retries SHALL have distinct occurrence identities.

### Requirement: causal layered party presentation
Rooms SHALL contain independent depth layers, authored guest floor slots and
bounded ambient, musical and gameplay reactions. Major social reactions SHALL
be paced to preserve challenger demonstrations and cue readability. Actual
audible arrangement state SHALL drive musical energy; requested future tiers
SHALL NOT immediately change it. Rendering SHALL NOT mutate gameplay state.

#### Scenario: readable living scene
Given an active response phrase, guests and props MAY react while controls and
judgment cues remain fixed and legible. Reduced motion SHALL preserve attention,
approval and passage-opening information with restrained authored poses.

### Requirement: presentation clock continuity
Music-linked animation SHALL freeze on pause and reconstruct from the music
timeline, including the continuing conductor during approval and bridges.

#### Scenario: approval across a bridge
Given a cleared session whose local beat stops advancing, approval and traversal
SHALL continue on the conductor timeline without changing completed judgments.

### Requirement: reusable seated audience animation
Every reusable guest SHALL have authored multi-frame idle, notice, seated rhythm,
standing, dance, clap and cheer clips, plus stand-up and sit-down transitions.
Furniture SHALL retain its floor anchor through those transitions. Outfit
variants SHALL preserve skin, hair, anatomy and alpha rather than tinting the
whole person.

#### Scenario: slowly recruited spectators
Given successful phrases, individual guests SHALL enter their next clip at
different bounded delays. A new result SHALL NOT cut an unfinished stand-up
transition into a seated or standing loop.

#### Scenario: restrained early room and final ovation
Given twelve guests, garden SHALL retain at least nine occupied guests even at
approval, while VIP MAY gradually earn all twelve standing and cheering before
approval. Clearance and optional style SHALL NOT override the room's audience
budget. These presentation ceilings SHALL NOT add a new mastery gate.

### Requirement: shared floor perspective
Background, floor anchors, actors and furniture SHALL use one source-to-world
cover transform. Actor scale SHALL derive from floor depth and a fixed standing
reference, shared across all clips. Mācron SHALL remain toy-sized relative to an
adult at the same distance. HUD and judgment geometry SHALL remain independent.

#### Scenario: seated guest rises at the same distance
Given a seated guest whose floor position is fixed, standing SHALL change the
authored pose without rescaling the head or moving the chair base. A farther
adult SHALL project smaller than an equivalent nearby adult.

### Requirement: challengers visibly perform the actual challenge
Each challenger SHALL demonstrate the actual active limb motif and musical note
timing, with anticipation, accents, follow-through and settling. The existing
musical rest SHALL contain finishing, ceding and inviting gestures. During the
player response the challenger SHALL watch without introducing competing cues.

#### Scenario: floor changes hands
Given the final demonstrated note, the challenger SHALL finish and invite Mācron
before the response's early input window. Mācron SHALL finish and return attention
to the challenger at the response boundary; accepted required inputs and optional
flourishes SHALL take visual priority over a cosmetic handover.

### Requirement: independently phased party life
Foliage, umbrellas, flames, drinks and waiters SHALL use bounded, independently
phased activity. Ambient lights SHALL remain gentle and non-strobing. Foreground
ambient clocks SHALL freeze during pause and backgrounding; reduced motion SHALL
retain steady lights and informative held poses.

#### Scenario: living room while nobody scores
Given an occupied audience and no scored inputs, ambient life MAY continue but
SHALL NOT create social interest, score, progress or a new judgment clock.
