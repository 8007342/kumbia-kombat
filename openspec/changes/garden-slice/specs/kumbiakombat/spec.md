## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Portrait rhythm prototype connects audio and combat

The app SHALL provide explicit audio activation, a portrait fight with four
limb regions, required taps, optional swipes and respect-driven arrangement
changes on the shared music transport. Camera motion SHALL not move controls.

#### Scenario: earning entry to the party

- **WHEN** the player mirrors all required response notes and passes the full-music proof
- **THEN** the challenger SHALL approve entry and the game SHALL offer the next implemented room
- **AND** missing an optional swipe SHALL never block progress
