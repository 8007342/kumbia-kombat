# Feel, 2026-11-08: wider windows, and the taps that were being lost

The owner, after playing act two on a phone: "still waaaay too punishing for early and late taps",
"I see mostly red", and "often times I don't see any tap detected at all" while still hearing the
tap's click. This follows `plan/feel-2026-11-07.md`.

## What was wrong

"Mostly red" means good-but-late, and the click with no ring is a stray tap. That combination points
to a steady late offset, not to windows that were too narrow. Reading the input path found four
ways that a tap landing in time, or a little late, got thrown away. All of them hit players who lean
late hardest, and some got worse after the player calibrated:

1. **The ring was chosen on the wrong clock.** `_resolveTap` picked the ring on the judged clock at
   handler time, while the judge used the touch's own time stamp. So a touch that was handled a frame
   late found its note's window already closed, and came out stray.
2. **The candidates were the drawn cues.** `visibleCues` drops a ring 200 ms after its beat is
   *heard*, on the audible clock. The judge's clock runs behind that by the touch delay (40 ms), the
   calibration and the auto-nudge. With a +150 ms calibration, a tap 50 ms late was already stray.
   The autoplay had the same bug, and a stronger nudge exposed it (see the page tests).
3. **Expiry ran before the tap.** `tap()` calls `advance()`, which expired notes at the current time
   before the tap was judged at its own, older time.
4. **One busy frame dropped the time stamp.** An event older than 150 ms lost its stamp, so it was
   judged when the handler ran, which is late.

On top of that, the auto-nudge could never fix a real phone offset. It was capped at 60 ms, and it
learned only from taps that landed inside the window. Taps from a phone more than about 200 ms late
were all stray, so it never saw them.

## Research

- StepMania's default judge (Judge 4) is Marvelous 22.5 ms, Perfect 45, Great 90, Good 135 and
  Boo 180, either way. Those are arcade pads with almost no input latency.
  ([StepMania forum](https://www.stepmania.com/forums/general-questions/show/586),
  [FFR thread](https://www.flashflashrevolution.com/vbz/showthread.php?t=80295))
- Rhythm Quest, a casual game, is deliberately lenient: "you can sometimes be a 16th note /
  125 milliseconds off in either direction and still get credit". It also has a leniency setting and
  a latency calibration. ([Devlog 14](https://rhythmquestgame.com/devlog/14.html),
  [Devlog 56](https://rhythmquestgame.com/devlog/56.html))
- Commercial touchscreen latency runs from about 50 to 200 ms
  ([arXiv 2408.02525](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.02525)). Google built WALT because touch and audio
  latency on Android vary by device and have to be measured
  ([Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2016/04/a-new-method-to-measure-touch-and-audio.html)).
- Bluetooth adds 150 to 300 ms (RTINGS, cited in the 2026-11-07 note). No window can absorb that;
  only an offset can.
- Designers' common advice is to judge on the touch's time stamp, not on frame time, and to calibrate
  the offset rather than widen windows without limit
  ([Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3434111928),
  [Rhythm Quest devlog 10](https://ddrkirbyisq.medium.com/rhythm-quest-devlog-10-latency-calibration-fb6f1a56395c)).

## What changed (`lib/gameplay/`)

| | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perfect | ±75 ms | ±90 | StepMania's Great, on a device whose touch alone varies by tens of ms |
| Early window | 180 | 220 | Wider than the widest arcade grade, for phone touch latency |
| Late window | 200 | 260 | Every unmeasured delay reads late, never early |
| Near miss | none | −400 / +500 | Aimed but outside the window: shown faint, counts for nothing, teaches the nudge |
| Auto-nudge cap | 60 | 250, as the tap-along | A phone's real offset, Bluetooth included |
| Auto-nudge step | half, over 40 ms | whole on the first move, then half, over 30 ms | A device offset is there from the first phrase |
| Event age kept | 150 ms | 200 in the session, 500 in the backend | A busy phone frame; stays under the early window |
| Note expiry | at the window's end | 120 ms later (`tapGraceMs`) | A touch already on its way still finds its note |

Fixes:

- Rings are picked from `GardenSession.tapCues`, on the judge's clock, at the touch's own time.
- A ring in its window always beats a near miss, so reach can never steal a hit.
- Autoplay uses `tapCues` too.

What stays the same:

- Charts keep only the old 180 ms clear (`guaranteedWindowMs`).
- A room's win is still decided when the last note's late window ends, so the next tier is asked
  for on time. That's now 60 ms later, so the golden's clear times moved by 60 ms and nothing else.
- The final swipe's wait uses `settleLateMs` (200, the old late window), so the exit, success music
  and bridge timings are unchanged.

## Checks

792 tests pass (789 before, plus new ones for near misses, reach and the nudge). `flutter analyze`
shows the 4 usual notes, and both music validators pass.

## What needs the owner's phone

1. Play any level without calibrating. Most taps should read gold. A late tap now shows a faint ember
   ring instead of nothing, and after one or two phrases the nudge should turn most reds gold.
2. If taps still read mostly red after three phrases, try `?latency=150` and `?latency=250` from the
   console and say which feels right. That's the phone's real offset, and it tells us whether the
   browser under-reports it.
3. With Bluetooth earbuds, the nudge now reaches 250 ms by itself. Calibrating with SYNC is still
   better.
4. Say whether the faint marks are useful or just noise.
