## The room

**Look** [P]: the mansion's grand ballroom seen straight down its length. Arched windows onto the night garden on the left behind burgundy drapes, an arched mirror on the right that repeats the windows (the "hall of mirrors" idea), gilded panels and sconces between, three crystal chandeliers in a row across the hall, a coffered ceiling, double doors in the middle of the far wall, and a dais with a black grand piano at the far left. Herringbone parquet, dark and calm in the tap band.

**How it was made** [F]: `rooms.py` route, two runs, 30 candidates in all.

| Run | Style reference | Candidates | What went wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| run-a | the indoor party (`cool-crowd`) | 6 first passes, 9 second passes | Lanterns everywhere besides the chandeliers (the party plate's lanterns, copied), the dais shrank to the left edge, and bright chandelier reflections landed in the tap band |
| run-b | the billiard room, newly listed in `style.json` `acceptedRooms` | 6 first passes, 9 second passes | First passes laid a pink carpet runner down the middle (the greybox's floor colour). Second passes cleared it; "the bare parquet runs from wall to wall" helped |

Changes between runs: the billiard room as style (one lamp per table, no lanterns), "the only lights in the room are the three crystal chandeliers", a wider dais "with open space beside it for two musicians", and "only the far half of the floor carries soft, faint reflections". The last one didn't work: every second pass still mirrored the chandeliers mid-floor, at y .59-.67, straddling the tap band's top. They are painted out as fills (below).

**Picking** [F, P]: local vision first (`tool/metrics/vision.py`, qwen2.5vl:7b), then my eyes on phone-crop sheets beside the garden, bar, party and billiard plates (`art_runs/rooms/salon/run-*/sheets/`).
- Local vision, asked per plate for counts (chandeliers, piano and side, people, lettering, doors, mirrors): it counted chandeliers as 3 or 4 on every run-a plate, which can't tell crystal chandeliers from the lanterns that were the real fault. It said "yes, piano, left" and "no people, no text" on all six, which was true. Its "no mirrors" on 990105 was right. So it confirmed what was easy and missed what mattered. About 4 s a plate.
- Picked **991002** (run-b, from first pass 990803): three chandeliers and no lanterns, the garden in both arches so the right one reads as a mirror of the left, doors centred and closed, the dais and piano clear, and the calmest floor of the 18 second passes. Runner-up 991102 had a busier diamond floor; 991100 had a door left ajar.
- **Weak point** [F]: the piano's keyboard faces the viewer and its bench sits at x .07-.15, so the pianist is at the phone's left edge (a 390x844 phone shows x .153-.847). A phone shows about half of her; wider screens show all of her. Same trade as the party's marimba.

## What went wrong

- My first layout put the far wall too deep and three chandeliers in a line down the centre, which stacked into one blob in the guide. I moved them into a row across the hall.
- The compiler refused off-phone guest marks; the spec keeps on-phone guests, and `marks.json` notes the rest.
- A rig skeleton of a seated pianist seen from behind read as a walking figure, so I dropped that route before spending GPU on it (see "The trio").
- `tool/art/pipeline/promote.py` declared `--new` twice, so it crashed on start. Fixed.
- `studio.run_workflow` only reads the main checkout, and this worktree is outside it. `tool/art/rooms/run_room_jobs.py` sends the billiards run's committed room graph with this job's prompt, prefix, size and LoRA as node overrides, after checking the graphs differ in nothing else.
