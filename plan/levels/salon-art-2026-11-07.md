## The room

**Look** [P]: the mansion's grand ballroom seen straight down its length. Arched windows onto the night garden on the left behind burgundy drapes, an arched mirror on the right that repeats the windows (the "hall of mirrors" idea), gilded panels and sconces between, three crystal chandeliers in a row across the hall, a coffered ceiling, double doors in the middle of the far wall, and a dais with a black grand piano at the far left. Herringbone parquet, dark and calm in the tap band.

**How it was made** [F]: `rooms.py` route, two runs, 30 candidates in all.

| Run | Style reference | Candidates | What went wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| run-a | the indoor party (`cool-crowd`) | 6 first passes, 9 second passes | Lanterns everywhere besides the chandeliers (the party plate's lanterns, copied), the dais shrank to the left edge, and bright chandelier reflections landed in the tap band |
| run-b | the billiard room, newly listed in `style.json` `acceptedRooms` | 6 first passes, 9 second passes | First passes laid a pink carpet runner down the middle (the greybox's floor colour). Second passes cleared it; "the bare parquet runs from wall to wall" helped |

Changes between runs: the billiard room as style (one lamp per table, no lanterns), "the only lights in the room are the three crystal chandeliers", a wider dais "with open space beside it for two musicians", and "only the far half of the floor carries soft, faint reflections". The last one didn't work: every second pass still mirrored the chandeliers mid-floor, at y .59-.67, straddling the tap band's top. They are painted out as fills (below).

**Picking** [F, P]: local vision first (`tool/metrics/vision.py`, qwen2.5vl:7b), then my eyes on phone-crop sheets beside the garden, bar, party and billiard plates (`art_runs/rooms/salon/run-*/sheets/`).
- Local vision, asked per plate for counts (chandeliers, piano and side, people, lettering, doors, mirrors): it counted chandeliers as 3 or 4 on every run-a plate, which can't tell crystal chandeliers from the lanterns that were the real fault. It said "yes, piano, left" and "no people, no text" on all six, which was true. Its "no mirrors" on 990105 was right. So it confirmed what was easy and missed what mattered. About 4 s a plate.
- Picked **991002** (run-b, from first pass 990803): three chandeliers and no lanterns, the garden in both arches so the right one reads as a mirror of the left, doors centred and closed, the dais and piano clear, and the calmest floor of the 18 second passes. Runner-up 991102 had a busier diamond floor; 991100 had a door left ajar.
- **Weak point** [F]: the piano's keyboard faces the viewer and its bench sits at x .07-.15, so the pianist is at the phone's left edge (a 390x844 phone shows x .153-.847). A phone shows about half of her; wider screens show all of her. Same trade as the party's marimba.

**Camera, refit to the paint** [F]: far wall foot at y .380, the centre joint and side-wall bases meet near x .505, doors 1.75 adults tall (leaves x .42-.60, top .187). That gives horizon .27 and eye 1.0 adult. Right-wall base agrees within a few pixels at t = 2.0-2.1. Mācron at heroHome (y .60) is 45 logical px against the garden's 38. The paint isn't metric: the dais and piano are drawn at about the floor's scale at their own image y, not raised, so the trio uses no platform lift. `ids.png` is regenerated from the refit spec but doesn't match the paint exactly; `marks.json` has a hand-traced floor outline for the level's test.

Black dinner jackets with champagne-gold lapels and bow ties, a uniform for the house's musicians [P]. White is Don Terciopelo's, so nobody else on the dais wears it. The trio stands on the dais at the far left, playing toward the room.

**New for instruments** [F]: mask-only garments `violin-body`, `violin-bow` and `bongo-pair` in `tool/art/rig/garments.py`, the same kind as the congas'. `band_finish.py` has lock rules and picks for both. The violin is locked by its varnish for place and the whole figure for size, because the bow changes the frame's height.

**The violin's bow barely moves** between drawings [F]. The words asked for the bow drawn out and pushed in, and all ten frames kept it at much the same angle. On a phone he's about 30 px tall, so the change shows mostly as his sway. If it reads as stiff, a code-drawn bow is the next step.

**The pianist** [F, P]: the plate's keyboard faces the viewer, so she sits with her back to the room. A seated rig skeleton seen from behind looked like a walking figure, so I didn't draw from it. Instead Qwen drew her straight onto a 256 by 320 crop of the plate at 3x (`tool/art/rooms/pianist.py`, `pianist.json`), so the bench, the piano and the light set her place and size. Each frame is an edit of the key. The layer is what differs from the plate inside a polygon around her, after undoing the whole-crop shift Qwen adds between edits (up to 10 px at 3x). Without that, the piano's lid showed a step at the polygon's edge. Her atlas is 1:1 with the plate when her feet sit at her anchor (plate px 110, 562), sized by the floor plane there: `playerHeightPx` 225. Frames: relaxed_a is the key, relaxed_b lifts her right hand in a small flourish, and swing_a and swing_c sway her head and shoulders left and right. tap_up_a, tap_up_b, swing_b and swing_d reuse those drawings, so the band ladder's names all exist.
- **Phone:** she sits at plate x .08-.19, and a 390x844 phone starts at .153, so a phone shows her shoulder and arm only. Wider screens show all of her. Fixing it means another plate, or repainting the piano further right.
- **Pixels:** her layer is the plate's own resolution, not palette-locked like the frame sprites, so it matches the painting rather than the other sprites.

**Local vision on the trio** [F]: hands, violins and bows counted on the 4 violin sheets: 2/1/1 on three, which was right, and "4 hands" on 990501, which was wrong (it has 2). Hands and drums on the 5 bongo picks: 2 and 2 each, which was right. It didn't catch the djembes in 990702 or the tails and gold waistcoat on violin 990502, because it wasn't asked. My eyes caught them.

**Sizes, from the composite proof** [P]: at stature 1 the bongo player and violinist dwarf the painted piano and the seated pianist, so `marks.json` asks for stature .85 for both.

## What went wrong

- My first layout put the far wall too deep and three chandeliers in a line down the centre, which stacked into one blob in the guide. I moved them into a row across the hall.
- The compiler refused off-phone guest marks; the spec keeps on-phone guests, and `marks.json` notes the rest.
- A rig skeleton of a seated pianist seen from behind read as a walking figure, so I dropped that route before spending GPU on it (see "The trio").
- `tool/art/pipeline/promote.py` declared `--new` twice, so it crashed on start. Fixed.
- `layers.py frames` only writes into a level file laid out on several lines; my one-line stand-in was silently left unchanged the first time.
- `studio.run_workflow` only reads the main checkout, and this worktree is outside it. `tool/art/rooms/run_room_jobs.py` sends the billiards run's committed room graph with this job's prompt, prefix, size and LoRA as node overrides, after checking the graphs differ in nothing else.
