# The curation console (/console) and the share page (/qr), 2026-11-07

Branch `work/1878-cnsl`. OpenSpec change: `openspec/changes/add-curation-console/`.

## What to try

On a phone, after the coordinator builds and previews:

1. Open `/console`. Six tabs: LEVELS, MUSIC, ROOMS, MOVES, CLOSE-UPS, NOTES.
2. **Levels:** each level has PLAY (that level alone), FLAWLESS (autoplay),
   OUTRO (tier 4 on autoplay: two phrases, then the close-up and walk out),
   MIXER, and tier buttons T0 to T4, plain and flawless. At the top: the whole
   campaign, campaign flawless, mixer, SYNC (opens the tap-along calibration),
   latency trials 0 to 200 ms (`?latency=`), the listening page and the QR page.
3. **Music:** play any take, success phrase or bridge. Shows BPM, bars, length,
   and a position bar you can drag.
4. **Rooms:** SHOW ROOM, then toggle animate (layers on the beat at the level's
   mid tempo, at full swing), layers, layer boxes (outlined with ids) and the
   floor overlay (horizon, one adult's height, Mācron's, the opponents' and the
   guests' marks).
5. **Moves:** filter by opponents, guests, band, background players, props,
   Mācron. Pick a tempo. SHOW CLIPS on an atlas, pick a clip: it plays at its
   data timing with `frame 3/8 · drawing name · beats`. Hold, step, mirror.
6. **Close-ups:** PLAY CLOSE-UP loops each level's close-up through the game's
   own `ExitShot.sample`, with the beat and the drawings on screen. Every
   close-up drawing is listed below with the json that uses it.
7. **Notes:** every card has a note box. COPY NOTES copies all of them, each
   under its reference and data path, ready to paste to us. Notes stay in that
   browser only.
8. Open `/qr`, and scan it from a second phone.

References look like `level / garden`, `music / garden / low`,
`garden / layer / fronds-left`, `cool-crowd / guest4 / occupied.pair`,
`gatekeeper / groove_basic`, `bar / closeup`, `closeup / nod-bb-rest`. So
"salon / terciopelo / bow frame 3 looks off" is a note we can act on.

## Facts, and how they were checked

- Everything is listed from the data files; nothing is hard-coded per level.
  A level file that ships, in the index or not, appears with its layers and
  clips. A broken file is shown as a problem and the rest still lists. Tested
  with a made-up level and a broken file (`test/console_catalog_test.dart`).
- Tests check that every level, every take with files, every atlas, every
  choreography clip, every run of drawings a level file names, every layer and
  every close-up drawing is listed, and that every file they point to exists.
- A widget test loads the real bundle at 360 px wide: no overflow, links open
  the right URLs, a note is kept and counted.
- The QR code encodes `https://kumbias.tlatoani.net` at error level Q (version
  3). A test checks every drawn module against the encoder and that the
  four-module quiet zone is light. zxing-cpp decodes the rendered picture as
  drawn, shrunk to 120 px and blurred (`tool/console/decode_qr.py`).
- `?tier=0..4` starts the first phrase at that tier (new, in
  `GardenSession.start`). `?sync=1` opens the SYNC calibration (new). Bad values
  are ignored.
- `flutter test`: 705 pass after merging FEEL. `flutter analyze lib test`: the
  4 usual notes.
- I looked at test renders of the QR page and the moves tab. Nobody has seen it
  in a real browser or on a phone, or heard the music player.

## Routing, and why

`web/console/index.html` and `web/qr/index.html` are copies of the app page with
the base fixed to `/`. The build copies them into `build/web/console/` and
`build/web/qr/`, so any static server, including the local preview, answers
`/console/`. Cloudflare redirects `/console` to `/console/` and serves it. Any
other path still falls back to `index.html` through the SPA setting. The app
picks its page from the first path segment, then `?page=console`, then
`#/console`. That gives three independent ways in. With only the SPA fallback,
the preview's static runtime would be left to chance. A test keeps the copies in
step with `web/index.html`.

## Proposals (reversible)

- Room layers are shown at full swing, the liveliest the room gets, not at
  the tier-dependent level the game shows early on.
- Drawings no clip names are offered as `<move>_*` groups and `all_frames`, at
  one beat a drawing, marked "timing assumed".
- Mācron's clips and the shared guests' crowd clips are sampled from the
  game's own functions (`heroSpriteFrame`, `ensembleClipFrame`, 2-beat pass).
- The QR page: dark ink on cream in a gold frame, the title in gold and cream,
  string lights, and seven of the cast grooving below at 104 BPM.
