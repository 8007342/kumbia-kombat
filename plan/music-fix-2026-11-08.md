# Music fix, 2026-11-08: the plan, on hold until the audio studio lands

**Status: waiting.** The owner decided on 2026-11-08 to re-render the music once the audio studio exists, not to patch the current files. Nothing in `assets/music/rendered/` has changed. Pick this up when macuahuitl-fedora reports that the studio can be reached from the forge. The measurements behind this note are in `plan/research/music-audit-2026-11-08.md`.

## What the owner heard

After playing act two on a phone: the high tiers of the bar, El Salón de Espejos, El Gran Comedor and La Galería "have lots of glitches, they sound like unfinished work". And across a level the tiers "feel a bit discontinuous between them, like three songs instead of one song growing up". The scores themselves were praised.

## What was measured (the audit)

- **The pipelines finished.** Every chain is complete, its hashes match, and the files rebuild bit-identically from the cached masters. No file is truncated, a placeholder or silent.
- **The high tiers lose their stereo image.** Each high is the 4th of four chained latent re-sampling passes at denoise 0.6 (ACE-Step 1.5 XL turbo), and each pass reduces the stereo correlation:

  | Room | Mid | High |
  |---|---|---|
  | Bar | +0.38 | −0.07, with 59 % of half-second windows anti-correlated |
  | Party (`cool-crowd`) | +0.51 | −0.35, the worst of all, though nobody reported it |
  | Galería | +0.69 | +0.20; its clipping also grows on each pass, 6 to 157 runs |
  | Comedor | +0.79 | +0.35 |
  | Salón | +0.58 | +0.38 |

  On headphones this sounds phasey, and on a phone speaker it partly cancels. The highs also get 2–3× brighter and lose 2–4 dB of bass. This is the likeliest cause of "glitchy".
- **No outro files exist.** Each success tag is cut from the high tier, so it inherits the stereo loss (bar, party and galería are the worst). The garden bridge is 2.6 LU quieter than the room it leads into.
- **"Three songs":**
  - Harmony is not the cause. The tiers are the same performance bar for bar (chroma cosine 0.97–0.99).
  - The causes are a tempo jump of +6 to +7 % at every tier change, and a sudden change of sound at mid → high: much brighter, thinner and wider.
  - The high tier sounds *different*, not *bigger*. Its onset density doesn't rise.

## The fix, once the studio is here

The studio arrives in three orders (macuahuitl-fedora, 2026-11-08):

1. **2055-grzz:** ffmpeg, sox, rubberband, the Python audio stack and a glitch detector (clicks, dropouts, spectral holes, clipping, loop seams). PR #1034 was in the landing queue on 2026-11-08.
2. **2055-d9sa:** Demucs stem separation and a tier tool, about a day after grzz. GPU torch on the host is unmeasured; CPU Demucs is the fallback.
3. **2055-dihg:** ACE-Step repaint, extend and lego/extract, after grzz.

Steps, in order:

1. **Gate on the detector first.** Run it on every current tier, tag and bridge, and keep the results as the "before". Add a stereo-correlation check (fail below about +0.2 overall, or when more than 10 % of windows are negative) and a clipping check to the music validators. Then a high tier like today's can't ship again.
2. **Build the tiers by layering, not by re-sampling.** Split the approved mid (one performance per room) into stems with Demucs. Then:
   - low = mid minus some layers, for example drums and bass with a thinned melody;
   - mid = the original;
   - high = mid plus added layers, loudness-matched, with the stereo image kept.

   Generate the extra layers with ACE-Step lego, or with repaint on the stem, never with chained full-mix passes. This is what makes "one song growing up": the same recording, getting denser.
3. **Shrink or remove the tempo steps.** Try one tempo for all three tiers of a room, so the build-up comes from layers alone. If a lift is still wanted, keep the steps to about 3 % and stretch with rubberband, not mixrs's 4-ms WSOLA. Check the billiards half-beat spacing (`hardest_combo_test`) after any tempo change.
4. **Recut the tags and bridges** from the new tiers. Check that each bridge's loudness is within 1 LU of the room it leads into. The success tags may want to come from mid plus a short ending rather than from the full high.
5. **Rooms in scope:** all nine. Worst first: bar, party, galería, comedor, salón, terraza (its clipping grows 33 → 682 runs), garden. Billiards measured clean but should be rebuilt the same way for consistency. The pool lawn still uses older takes and its own entry table, so treat it last.
6. **Owner listens** on a listening page before anything is promoted. Then rebuild with `tool/music/mixrs`, update `assets/music/rendered/manifest.json` (loop points, LUFS), run the validators and the tests, and go through `work` → `game` → `main`.
