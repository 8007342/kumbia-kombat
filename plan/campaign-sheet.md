## What this table shows

- **The run gets too long.** Adding the pool brings a clean run to about 8:37, against the 5-8 minute target, and every new level adds more. It also invalidates ranked times. The design research recommends acts of 3-4 levels with their own boards. Owner's decision.
- **Rewards build toward the finale.** Fist bump, then oil, then a nod, a key, then the full high five. Nothing before the finale should use a glint, caption or close-up at that scale.
- **The HUD's "/ 05" is a stated number** (`displayTotal` in `levels/index.json`), so it needs a new answer once acts are decided.
- **Signature step** comes from the salsa teacher. It's established early and paid off at `cool-crowd`.
