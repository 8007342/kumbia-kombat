# Signature moves from a teacher's video

Draft, 2026-11-06. Nothing has been filmed or tried. The owner will ask the salsa teacher.

## What the video gives us

The video's job is the real move: timing, weight, arms and feet. A cutout alone doesn't make game art. The route is teacher video, then chosen key poses and their beats, then redrawn as our characters in the sprite pipeline.

Mācron is the hard part. He has no human skeleton, so pose tools don't suit him, and his side views failed in the earlier pilot. Expect the moves to transfer easily to the human dancers, and to need adapting for him. The first clip will test that.

## Shooting guide

- **Background:** a plain wall or sheet, and open shade or overcast light, as the owner proposed. Clothing should contrast with the background, so avoid white on white.
- **Phone:** on a tripod or propped up, with no zoom or panning. Lock focus and exposure. 1080p at 60 fps, or 120 for fast spins. Turn HDR off.
- **Framing:** the whole body with room around it, including feet and raised hands, at about hip height.
- **Clothes:** fitted enough to show the knees and hips, with visible shoes.
- **Each move:** start and end in a neutral stance, once slowly, then at full tempo. Count aloud or clap at the start, so timing can be recovered.
- **Views:** front first. Back and side if the teacher has time. Spins benefit most.
- **Permission:** written consent to use the moves and footage to make game art, and how the teacher wants to be credited.

Send one 10-second test clip first, so the route can be tested before the teacher records the rest.
