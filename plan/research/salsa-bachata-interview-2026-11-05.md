# Interview guide: salsa and bachata teacher

Goal: get real dance knowledge into Kumbia Kombat, a one-thumb rhythm game where a tiny robot, Mācron,
earns his way through a mansion party by copying each dancer's moves. The player only taps four limbs:
left hand, right hand, left foot, right foot.

If you only have ten minutes, ask the starred ones.

## 1. Mācron's signature step
- ★ If a tiny, cheerful, slightly clumsy robot had one signature move, what would it be? Could you show it?
- ★ Can you count it out? On which beats do the feet and hands move?
- What makes it read from far away, as a small silhouette? What would make it look wrong?
- How would it grow as he gets better: same step in a beginner, a confident and a showing-off version?
- May we credit you for it, and how would you like to be named?

## 2. How a dance is built
- ★ What does a good social dance look like over a whole song: where does it stay simple, build and peak?
- ★ What are the basic building blocks, from smallest to largest? What do you call them?
- What does a dancer do between "big" moves? We show too few moves today, and it reads as stiff.
- How does a dancer show they've heard a change in the music: a break, a horn line, the percussion?
- What do you teach first, and what tells you a student has really got it?

## 3. Moves for our four limbs
- ★ Which salsa and bachata moves clearly feature one hand or one foot, so a player could copy them?
- ★ Which moves look great but are all body, like hips and shoulders, and can't be mapped to one limb?
- Bachata's tap on the fourth beat, and salsa's break step: would those make good moments to tap?
- Can you give us 5 to 8 moves per style, from easy to hard, with their real names?

## 4. Two dancers, one challenge
- ★ Our new level has two women dancing together who stop the robot. What do they do? Side by side, mirrored, or trading moves?
- ★ How do dancers "call and answer" each other in a real circle or shine? Is there a tradition for this?
- How would two friends challenge a newcomer playfully, without any cruelty?
- How would they signal "okay, you've earned it"? And what does a warm welcome look like at the end?

## 5. Flourishes
- ★ What are the optional flourishes: spins, styling, hand and hair? Which feel earned rather than showy?
- When is a spin good musicality, and when is it just noise?
- Which small touches instantly make someone look like they can really dance?

## 6. Music
- ★ Which tempo feels right for a beginner, a confident dancer and a peak moment, in each style?
- What must a track have so that you feel it's really salsa or bachata? What gives a fake away?
- Which beat do you dance on (salsa on 1 or on 2)? Which is easiest for people new to it?
- Could we send you game music to react to?

## 7. Respect and tone
- ★ What do games and films get wrong about these dances? What would make you wince?
- How do we show close, sensual dances in a game teens might play, so they stay classy?
- What should we know about where these dances come from, and who to credit?
- What do dancers actually wear at a nice party, and what shoes? What would look like a costume?

## 8. Before you go
- Could you record short clips of the signature step and the key moves? Front and side views, counted aloud, would help us most.
- May we use those clips as reference for the animation? They wouldn't be published.
- Who else should we talk to?
